# Changelog

What changes in each version of The Agent Office, written for the people who use it. [Türkçe](CHANGELOG_TR.md)

Versions are three numbers, MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH, and stay at 0.x while The Agent Office is young. The Microsoft Store shows the same version with a fourth number, which is always 0 (0.9.0 is 0.9.0.0 in the Store).

## 0.9.0 (not released yet)

The first public version.

### The island: Octolantis

- A small island at the top of the screen. It opens when the pointer reaches it and closes again when the pointer moves away.
- It shows who is working, who is idle and who is waiting for you. Questions and permission requests always come first.
- Octo, a little octopus, lives in the island. Octo waves when the island opens, follows the pointer with its eyes, and calls you while a chat is waiting for your answer.
- The island speaks up only for things that need you. Finished replies and the team's own messages stay quiet.
- Pages you can turn with a sideways swipe: media controls with optional synced lyrics, notes and to-dos with reminders, a focus timer and countdown, alarms, a water reminder, and a file shelf for dragging files between apps.
- At rest the small island shows the time, how many agents are working, the battery and, if you like, the weather for your city.
- A glass or a dark look, and a different resting look for the desktop, for apps and for the browser.
- It steps aside for real full-screen apps, follows your monitor and its scaling, and can be moved along the top edge.
- A limits panel with Claude's and Codex's 5-hour and weekly usage, and when each resets.

### The office

- Every project is an office and every chat is an agent at a desk. The offices follow the project and chat lists of Claude and Codex.
- The manager sits at the big desk at the head of the room, with the team in front. A click on the big desk changes the manager, and each office offers the choice once.
- Messages fly between the manager and the teams, and a chat's card shows its conversation in plain words.
- A living office: agents' short lines in speech balloons, Lucy the dog, the cat, the parrot and the aquarium, coffee breaks and lunch, and a window with the real time of day, weather and season. It can be turned off for a calm, plain office.
- A click on an agent opens that chat in Claude or Codex. The Agent Office never answers a question or approves a permission for you.

### Getting started

- Works with Claude Code (terminal, IDE and the Claude desktop app), Codex (CLI and desktop app) and Cursor.
- A first-run screen finds your tools and connects them in one step. Settings show each connection as it is, with one repair button and a way to remove it.
- An optional skill pack for working with a manager chat and its teams, installed, updated or removed from Settings.
- Starts quietly with Windows if you want it to, with a tray icon for the office, Settings and mute.
- 11 languages: English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Português (Brasil), Русский, 日本語, 한국어, 简体中文.
- Everything stays on your PC: no account, no ads, no analytics, no server of ours.
