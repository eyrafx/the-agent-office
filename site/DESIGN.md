# Product site direction

Keep the Windows dynamic island and the visual AI agent office equally visible. Use the actual desktop interface for the interactive preview, with a browser adapter and fictional data.

The marketing shell uses charcoal (#10131c), paper (#f1ece4), cyan (#91d6d8), coral (#ffa98c) and lilac (#c0b6e5). Segoe UI Variable Display and Text use system fallbacks; no commercial fonts are distributed.

The opening pairs left-aligned product messaging with a live, sharply rendered compact island above the original office scene. The Windows desktop wallpaper provides a recognisable setting in both this overview and the interactive workspace. Nine icon-and-label rectangular buttons form a horizontal feature rail, with horizontal scrolling on small screens. Island features are organised in an expandable guide. The office has a distinct question hand-off story with three sequential steps. A language section lists the 11 interface languages verified against the desktop app's LANGUAGES registry and JSON catalogs. Installer status and FAQs close the page.

Avoid the reference site's layout, coloured square-icon matrix, ribbon wallpaper, companion section sequence and Store/download block. Product imagery must come from AgentOffice itself. The composition is built around the two connected product views.

Capsule preview sizing preserves separate space for the artwork, clock and status. The longer date-and-time appearance expands the closed capsule to 232 CSS pixels instead of letting its contents overflow.

Do not introduce unverified performance figures, download counts, Store links or release claims. The installer is not yet linked.

The website supports the app's 11 languages. A globe menu in the upper right and native-language buttons in the language section share one preference. English is the default; an explicit language URL or a saved browser preference overrides it. All marketing copy, FAQ content, labels, demo captions, document metadata and accessible control names use bundled translations. The browser product adapter reads the same language through its URL and uses the product's existing catalogs. Example project names, filenames and sample song lyrics remain sample content. No external translation service is called.

The hero now uses two independently framed product studies, with clear space between them. The Windows study is narrower and right aligned; the office is wider. Captions use the same external label system. No joined seams, stacked pseudo-windows, repeated mascot footer, or in-image marketing labels.

The compact island uses the desktop app's current status-strip component and its native five-second clock/status rotation. The sample laptop charges at 68%, the sample audio output is AirPods Pro, and the sample weather is Istanbul at 21°C. Charging and AirPods toolbar actions play the original connection notices; weather shows its original city/temperature treatment. Manual examples receive a complete five-second turn, then native rotation resumes. Both the hero and main preview use this component. No device detection, geolocation or weather request is made by the website. New descriptions and controls are available in all 11 languages.

Show the app's verified first-launch glass appearance by default. Feed its actual glass renderer a local copy of the illustrated desktop, without capturing the visitor's screen. Keep the hero's whole frame available so the mascot and shoulders are never cropped. The main preview supports horizontal dragging by the original dotted grip, a short opt-in gesture demonstration, and keyboard movement; constrain the open panel to the desktop. Surface thin-line breathing and its five native colours directly beside the preview. Add an editorial gallery of the real menus (focus, water, notes and settings), with native screenshots and links to try them. Keep the two-product hero, existing language controls and privacy boundary intact.

## Kawaii redesign — 31 Oct 2026
The user chose direction C ("Kira-kira", manga sticker pop) from four hero studies. A night theme was built and then dropped at the user's request: the site is day only. Every section, FAQ, privacy, disclaimer and 404 speaks one art language: ink linework, sticker shadows that press in, pink screentone dots, candy colours, sections as slightly tilted manga panels, and sakura petals. Premium Japanese editorial kawaii rather than a toy: serious content (wallet addresses, network notes, the SmartScreen notice) stays plain and highly legible.
- Palette: lemon paper #fff6d2, paper #fffdf4, ink #2a1640, candy #ff5fa2, candy-soft #ffc4dc, mint #8fe8cf, sky #a9d8ff, grape #b59cff. Text pairs meet WCAG AA.
- Type: Dela Gothic One (display) and M PLUS Rounded 1c (text); Do Hyeon / Gowun Dodum for Korean and ZCOOL KuaiLe for Chinese. All SIL OFL, self-hosted as subsets in `dist/fonts/kawaii` (`kawaii-fonts.py`); CJK files hold only the characters the site uses and load only through unicode-range. No font service is contacted.
- Hero: copy on the left; the buttons are three rows of equal width (Windows / macOS / demo + support). Octo (the pink robot octopus, `dist/assets/octo`) is a die-cut sticker on a speed-line band; he waves hello, rests, blushes when the cursor lands on him and pleads while pressed, and the band changes colour with him (mint, candy pink when shy, lilac when pleading). Under it, the Octolantis story card and a three-panel manga strip retelling it.
- The hero island stays the real product frame and stays dark. Its 864x480 frame is see-through; while closed, only the area around the small island takes the pointer (clip-path), so the copy, buttons and Octo under the frame stay clickable. Hover timing is unchanged.
- Live demo: cream window with candy tab headers, chunky chips and ink borders; the wallpaper and the island inside stay as the product draws them. Feature views sit on one light pastel stage (pink, lilac, sky) with a soft shadow under the dark island.
- The old dark cards are paper and ink now: the crypto wallet (white QR in an ink frame, addresses in plain monospace on white), the developer note (a signed letter on ruled paper), download and Store cards (the SmartScreen notice as an amber-edged note), the task-manager card and stats, the "only on desktop" scenes (pastel screens; the island mock-ups stay dark like the app), contacts and the feedback form.
- Support: "Say thanks" makes the support Octo blush and say thank you; below it, "Star on GitHub" and "Leave a thank-you message" (GitHub Discussions; the dedicated thank-you thread still has to be created). Nothing is sent or counted.
- Sakura: `dist/sakura.js`, canvas, pre-rendered sprites, capped counts (hero 44 + 8, elsewhere 20), resolution capped at 1.5x; the page layer rests while the hero is on screen; everything stops off screen, in hidden tabs and under reduced motion.
- The "Coming soon" pill of the Windows buttons is in the markup (support.js removes it once a download is published), so the hero does not reflow after load.
- "The Agent Office" (`#agent-office`, after the office demo): the living office pitched as fun while you build, with seven cards (characters, pets, letters in the air, Agent of the Month, the click-to-play game layer, daily life, everything optional with simple mode). Copy in `office-copy.mjs` (18 messages × 11 languages). Videos are slots, not files: set `data-src` (and `data-poster`) on `.kw-office-film` and on the `.kw-loop` spans in the pets, letters and game cards (in `kawaii-markup.mjs` or directly in `dist/index.html`); `kawaii.js` then plays them muted and looping only while on screen (poster and controls under reduced motion). Without a source the illustrated placeholder stays.
- Files: `dist/kawaii.css` (loaded last, everything under `html.kw`, set in the markup), `dist/kawaii.js`, `dist/sakura.js`, `kawaii-markup.mjs` (idempotent markup changes), `kawaii-copy.mjs` (12 messages × 11 languages) and `office-copy.mjs`, both wired into `prepare-i18n.mjs`.

Verified (Playwright + installed Chrome, real mouse and touch): 5 pages × 11 languages × 1440/1280/768/375 px with no sideways scroll or cut text (320 px also checked for the header and hero buttons); computed-style audit of every visible text and every hover and keyboard-focus state outside the product views: no pair under WCAG AA, every focus stop visible, no leftover dark surfaces; hero island at DPR 1/1.25/1.5, 1440/1280: opens on hover, 0 changes while the pointer stays inside, closes ~150-280 ms after leaving; phone: tap opens and stays, tap elsewhere closes; Octo poses and band colours, thank-you, reduced motion (petals still) checked; CLS 0 under 4x CPU slowdown and a slow network; no console errors.
