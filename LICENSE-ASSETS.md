## Where the mascot comes from

The Octo mascot was created by the author, eyra, in October 2026: the stills with ChatGPT's image generation (OpenAI) from the author's own prompts and earlier robot-octopus concept, and the animated clips with a locally run open video model from that artwork. It is not based on any third-party character or stock artwork.
